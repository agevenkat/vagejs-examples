# A budget for shared homes

## The problem

Flatmates kept a shared spreadsheet that one person maintained and everyone else ignored. At the end of each month, someone did the maths, and someone else disagreed with it.

## What we tried

Our first version showed every expense and every person's share. It was accurate and nobody read it. In interviews, people only wanted to know one thing: do I owe money, and how much?

## What shipped

- **One number on the home screen:** what you owe, or what you're owed, updated after every new expense.
- **Bills that repeat.** Rent and internet are entered once and split the same way every month.
- **Settle up, not pay up.** The app suggests the fewest transfers to make everyone even, instead of a payment for every expense.

## What I learned

Fairness matters more than precision. Letting people mark an expense as "split however we agreed" did more for trust than any rounding rule.
