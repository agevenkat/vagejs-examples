# I design calm software for complicated services.

I'm Mira, an independent product designer. For eleven years I've worked with small teams on the apps people use when they're in a hurry: catching a tram, splitting the rent, remembering to water the fern. I like the unglamorous parts best, like error messages, empty states and the moment someone gets it right the first time.
