## Good to know

- **Eggless:** the ragi sourdough, white sourdough, pav, coconut and jaggery loaf and masala puff contain no egg. Ask us about the cakes.
- **Allergies:** we bake with wheat, milk, nuts and sesame in the same kitchen. Please tell us about any allergy before you order.
- **Whole cakes** need two days' notice. See [how to order](/order/).
