# Order ahead

## By phone

Call us on **080 0000 0000** between 9 am and 6 pm, Tuesday to Sunday. (This is an example number for this fictional bakery, not a real line.)

Tell us what you'd like, the day you'll collect it, and a name. We'll repeat the order back to you and keep it on the shelf behind the counter.

## At the counter

Ask any of us when you visit. For bread, ordering the day before is enough. You pay when you collect.

## Celebration cakes

Whole cakes need **two days' notice**. When you call, we'll ask about:

1. The flavour, from the cakes on our [menu](/menu/).
2. The size: 500 g serves about 6 people, 1 kg about 12.
3. Any writing on top (up to 20 characters).
4. Allergies or eggless needs.

A whole cake starts from ₹1,200. We'll tell you the exact price on the phone before you confirm.

## Collecting your order

Orders wait for you until closing time on the day you chose. Find us at the address on the [visit page](/visit/).
