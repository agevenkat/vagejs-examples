# Deepavali hours and sweet bread orders

We're open over the Deepavali weekend with slightly shorter hours: **6 am to 2 pm on Saturday and Sunday**. We're closed on Monday as usual, so we can rest and start baking again on Tuesday.

## Gift loaves

Our coconut and jaggery loaf makes a good gift: it keeps well for four days and travels easily. This week we're wrapping it in paper with a cotton string at no extra cost.

Order by Thursday evening for collection on Saturday. Call us or ask at the counter; [here's how ordering works](/order/).

Happy Deepavali from all four of us.
