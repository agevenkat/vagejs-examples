# Visit us

## Getting here

- **On foot:** from Sampige Road, turn into 8th Cross and take the first lane on the left. We're the shop with the blue shutter.
- **By metro:** the nearest stations are on the Green Line; it's a short walk from either.
- **Parking:** there's space for a few two-wheelers outside. Cars are easier on the main road.

## Good to know

- We take cash and UPI.
- The counter is step-free, and there are two small tables if you'd like to eat here.
- Mornings are busiest at weekends between 8 and 10 am.
